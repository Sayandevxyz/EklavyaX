Faction Wars Rules & Anti-Gaming Integrity

1. How are House Points Earned?

Every verified STEM Quiz question answered correctly contributes its full XP value directly to your House's live battle tally. Wagered 1v1 Peer Challenges award bonus XP to the victor's faction.

2. Anti-Gaming Server Verification

All scores are recorded and validated server-side. Correct answers submitted faster than the minimum human reading threshold (< 2s cooldown) or exceeding daily earn caps receive 0 XP and write to the security audit log without contributing to the battle.

3. Battle Finalization & Reward Payouts

When the battle timer reaches zero, the House with the highest aggregated XP is declared the Victor. Active contributors in the winning House receive +50 EduCoins & +100 XP. Contributors in all other participating Houses receive a guaranteed +15 EduCoins & +25 XP participation bonus.

4. House Balancing

New students are dynamically sorted into houses with the fewest active members to ensure balanced competition across all four Houses.
